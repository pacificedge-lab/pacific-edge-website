# Content Governance

Every public content object carries a publication status.

- `draft`
- `review`
- `approved`
- `published`
- `archived`

Production pages render only `published` entries. Development renders non-archived entries so content can be reviewed in context.

Partners are additionally controlled by an explicit publication flag. Partner content should never appear publicly until the relationship has been approved for representation.

Work content should be reviewed for confidentiality before publication.

Case studies use general organization descriptions. Client, employer, vendor and partner names, exact dates, counts and distinctive identifying details are excluded from public copy unless separately cleared for use. Internal evidence notes stay outside website source.

Earlier professional experience is attributed to Chris McLean; it must not imply that Pacific Edge delivered an assignment undertaken in a previous role. Work entries carry optional `organization` and `attribution` fields, displayed consistently on cards and full accounts. Review accounts and their links appear only in review builds until their status is changed to `published`.
