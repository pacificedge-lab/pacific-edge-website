Most organizations begin AI adoption by asking which tools or use cases to deploy. That is usually not the most important first question.

AI enters an operating system that already contains workflows, decision rights, information, incentives, governance and habits. The technology can amplify that system, but it rarely repairs it by itself.

Pacific Edge approaches AI readiness as an organizational capability question: can the surrounding system make useful, safe and repeatable use of what the technology provides?
