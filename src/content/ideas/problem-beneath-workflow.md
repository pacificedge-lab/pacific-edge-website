The visible workflow is not always the problem.

A slow, frustrating or heavily manual process may be expressing something deeper: unclear ownership, missing evidence, conflicting decision rules, inaccessible information or technology built around an obsolete assumption.

Automating the visible sequence before understanding those conditions can simply make the wrong system run faster.
