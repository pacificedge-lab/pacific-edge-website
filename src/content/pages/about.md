An organization can have capable people, valuable information and considerable activity while the pieces still fail to connect. Pacific Edge examines those connections and helps leadership determine what should change.

Clients can engage the practice for a focused assignment, implementation support or continuing advice and leadership. The work should leave the organization better able to understand its situation, make decisions and act on them.

## Chris McLean

Chris is a business consultant and former Director of Business Optimization whose experience spans operations, analytics, technology and organizational change.

His work has crossed education, healthcare, finance and telecommunications infrastructure. It includes examining difficult operational problems, developing practical systems and helping significant initiatives regain direction.

Chris looks across the decisions, incentives, information and working arrangements that shape a situation. That breadth helps him connect different perspectives and identify a response that fits the organization's actual circumstances.

## How we approach the work

We establish what is happening before prescribing a response. We examine the system behind a recurring problem and make the important choices clear.

We explain what the evidence supports, where uncertainty remains and how professional judgment informs the recommendation.

A practical outcome may be a decision, an operating approach or a technical system. It should be supported by the knowledge and ownership your organization needs to carry it forward.

## How the work comes together

Each engagement establishes the need, intended result and responsibilities. Chris leads work within his remit, with specialist contributions and delivery arrangements defined according to the assignment.

Advisory supports decisions, organizational improvement and leadership. Labs explores methods and technologies, validates new capabilities and develops reusable tools or products. What is learned can strengthen future work across the practice.
