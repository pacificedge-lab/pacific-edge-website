Pacific Edge helps districts improve how work gets done. We connect people, processes and information to reduce unnecessary effort, strengthen internal capability and support better decisions.

## Workforce and HR operations

Clarify the work behind absence management, return to work, case coordination and workforce reporting. We help connect information, establish responsibilities and improve continuity. Workforce and succession planning can also help leaders understand capacity, anticipate gaps and set practical priorities.

**Potential benefits**

- Clearer case ownership and more consistent follow-up
- More useful information for workforce decisions
- Stronger continuity when roles or staff change

## Organizational and process improvement

Understand where work stalls, handoffs become difficult and administrative effort accumulates. We map current processes, clarify accountability and redesign how work moves across teams—with recommendations grounded in your district’s systems, capacity and operating context.

**Potential benefits**

- Fewer avoidable handoffs and less rework
- Clearer responsibilities and decision points
- More consistent service delivery across teams

## Technology, automation and AI

Identify where existing tools, practical automation or purpose-built applications can improve everyday work. Technology choices start with operational needs. AI opportunities are considered alongside privacy, reliability, human judgment and the capacity to maintain what is introduced.

**Potential benefits**

- Less repetitive administrative effort
- Better-informed technology and adoption priorities
- Tools staff can use, understand and maintain

## JEIS case management in development

Joint Early Intervention Services depends on reliable case information, timely follow-up and coordination across people and processes.

Pacific Edge is developing a purpose-built case management application for a BC school district’s JEIS workflow. The focus is case visibility, follow-up coordination, administrative consistency and management oversight.

The intended benefits are clearer information, more reliable follow-up and less manual coordination: process design and practical technology working together.

This is a development example. Operational outcomes remain to be validated. Applying the approach in another district would begin with its workflows, systems, privacy requirements and operating responsibilities.

## A focused place to start

An engagement can begin with a review of one process that is taking more effort than it should.

Together, we establish how the work happens today, where effort or information is being lost, and which improvements are worth making. The review can provide:

1. A shared picture of the workflow, responsibilities and systems involved.
2. Prioritized improvements, with dependencies and trade-offs made clear.
3. Agreed next steps, ownership and implementation considerations.

Support can extend into process redesign, technology development or implementation. The aim throughout is to leave your team with usable tools, clearer processes and the knowledge to carry improvements forward.
