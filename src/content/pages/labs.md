Pacific Edge Labs explores new methods and technologies, tests important assumptions and develops capabilities that can be applied repeatedly. The work can support client delivery, strengthen internal systems, develop into a product or continue as a focused research effort.

## From exploration to dependable use

Early work tests whether an idea deserves further investment. As a capability develops, attention shifts to realistic use, reliability and the conditions under which it can be depended upon.

Each public project describes its purpose, current stage and intended use. A working demonstration, a validated capability and a released product represent different levels of maturity.
