## The challenge

A greenfield fibre-network business needed a shared operating framework across multiple partner companies. The starting point was a blank page: establish what activities constituted a fibre-network build, how those activities connected and how the businesses would work together.

The knowledge needed to define the operation was distributed across the partners. Each contribution had to fit into a common understanding of the full delivery chain and the management group company's oversight role.

## The contribution

Chris developed a bespoke framework with input from the participating businesses. The scope ran from business-case development and analytics through network design, engineering, construction, testing and turn-up, and go-to-market.

The framework connected activities, responsibilities, handoffs and management requirements across the chain. It provided a shared reference for the partners and a structure for the management group company to oversee and coordinate the operation.

## The result

The completed operating framework gave the partners a practical basis for understanding their contribution within the wider operation. It translated knowledge held across separate businesses into an explicit model of how the delivery ecosystem would work together.
