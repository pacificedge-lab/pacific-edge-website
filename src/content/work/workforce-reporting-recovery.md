## The challenge

A major provincial health organization urgently needed to implement enterprise vacation planning. During implementation, important differences emerged between the software's assumptions and the organization's requirements. Changing course was no longer an available option.

Reporting was a major difficulty for the organization, the union and government stakeholders. The software also struggled with seniority allocations and employee preferences. Accurate source information, including vacation entitlements and job groups, required substantial work by the data team.

## The contribution

The team secured a raw data export from the vendor. The extract was messy and unwieldy, so Chris authored an R-based processing solution and ran it on a dedicated machine set up for the work.

That work made the vendor extract usable for the reporting task. The data team's work on source information and Chris's processing solution addressed different parts of the information problem.

## The result

The processing solution made it possible to report on participation in the first wave of vacation planning. It also supplied data to workforce forecasting models, helping the organization identify expected pressure points and begin planning responses.

This recovered information the organization needed for both implementation reporting and proactive workforce planning within the existing software constraints.
